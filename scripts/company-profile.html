Who we are

About us

One contractor, accountable end to end

Sahil is the trading name of Sahil Contracting Co., a Jeddah-based general contractor working across residential, commercial, industrial and institutional projects in Saudi Arabia. We take a project from structure through to finishes, joinery and furniture — the whole of the work a building needs before anyone can move into it.

Because building, finishing, interiors and project management sit inside one company, the client signs one contract and holds one party responsible. Around twenty engineers and a site workforce of roughly two hundred deliver against a published programme, with progress reported against that programme rather than described in general terms, and a one-year warranty on completed works.
